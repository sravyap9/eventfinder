PRIVACY

Sidequests is a personal project that lists public events. Short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no cookies.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved events, city choice, quiz answers and ratings are kept in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. Nothing leaves your device for this. Clearing your browser data removes it.

What is sent anywhere

  • By default, nothing. The page only downloads its own files from this site.
  • If the quiz page asks to help improve recommendations and you press Yes, your quiz answers and each thumbs up or down (with the event it was about) are sent to a database. They are tied to a random id made in your browser, not to your name, email, IP address or device. Press No thanks and nothing is sent.
  • Those ratings are used only to train the recommendation model. To have a session deleted, open an issue on the project's GitHub page with the random id (it is stored in your browser as sq2_session).

Third parties

  • Fonts are hosted on this site, so no request goes to a font service.
  • Event links open the original event page on another site (Eventbrite, Luma, Do312 and so on). Those sites have their own privacy practices.
  • The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary server logs.

About the event data

Event details come from public pages of other sites and can be out of date or wrong. Always check the original event page before you go. Phone numbers and email addresses found in event descriptions are removed.
